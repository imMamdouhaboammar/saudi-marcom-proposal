# Crisis and Reputation Module

## Activate for

Crisis preparedness, issues management, reputation management, crisis communications, media response, social escalation, command centers, executive advisory, simulations, and recovery planning.

## Service layers

1. preparedness
2. monitoring and early warning
3. severity classification
4. governance and escalation
5. response content
6. spokesperson/executive support
7. live incident coordination
8. stakeholder communication
9. recovery and learning

Separate retainer coverage from incident surge work unless the contract explicitly bundles both.

## Discovery

Resolve:
- risk universe and critical stakeholders
- monitoring sources
- languages
- operating hours
- severity taxonomy
- decision authority
- legal/compliance review path
- spokesperson ownership
- channels and publishing permissions
- acknowledgement vs advisory vs publishing SLA
- out-of-hours escalation
- war-room activation conditions
- incident duration assumptions

## SLA model

Never use "real time" as a vague promise.

Define:
- monitoring coverage hours
- source refresh expectation
- alert threshold
- acknowledgement SLA
- analyst validation SLA
- escalation SLA
- advisory SLA
- holding-statement target where contractually appropriate
- approval dependency
- out-of-hours staffing

SLA clock should state when it starts, pauses, and ends.

## Deliverable units

| Deliverable | Unit | Acceptance examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| crisis framework | document | taxonomy, roles, workflows, templates |
| risk register | version | risk, severity, owner, trigger |
| simulation | exercise | scenario, participants, injects, after-action report |

## Safety and evidence

Keep separate:
- confirmed facts
- allegations
- sentiment signals
- rumors/unverified claims
- analyst interpretation

Do not amplify misinformation unnecessarily. Human approval remains required for sensitive external response unless a specific approved automation policy exists.

## Staffing and commercial logic

24/7 can require:
- multi-shift coverage
- supervisor/on-call escalation
- redundancy
- leave/absence coverage
- surge capacity

One analyst on business hours cannot commercially support a 24/7 promise.

Price separately:
- setup/preparedness
- recurring monitoring/advisory
- simulations
- spokesperson training
- incident surge
- after-hours coverage
- third-party monitoring platform

## Failure modes

Block or revise when:
- 24/7 is promised without staffing
- "sentiment" is treated as objective truth
- response authority is unclear
- legal approval dependencies are ignored
- monitoring and crisis teams duplicate the same analyst cost with no shared-resource logic
- incident surge has no commercial treatment
