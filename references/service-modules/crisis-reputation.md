# Crisis and Reputation Module

## Activate for

Crisis preparedness, issues management, reputation management, crisis communications, media response, social escalation, war rooms, executive advisory, and recovery planning.

## Workstreams

- risk and issue inventory
- monitoring and early warning
- severity and escalation model
- stakeholder map
- response governance
- holding statements and response templates
- spokesperson and simulation support
- live incident coordination
- post-crisis recovery and learning

## SLA precision

Never promise "real time" without defining coverage and human staffing.

Specify:

- monitoring hours
- source coverage
- alert threshold
- severity definitions
- acknowledgement SLA
- escalation owner
- advisory SLA
- out-of-hours coverage

## Quality and safety

Separate facts, allegations, sentiment signals, and unverified claims. Human review is required before sensitive external responses unless an explicitly approved automation policy exists.

## Pricing implications

Separate preparedness/setup, recurring monitoring/advisory, simulation, and live incident surge work. If 24/7 coverage is promised, the staffing and price must support it.
