# Benchmark Provenance

Classes: buyer authority, official public authority, bidder-owned evidence, credible public benchmark, private prior proposal pattern, and explicit assumption.

Every benchmark records source ID, title, locator, provenance class, checked date, confidentiality, allowed uses, prohibited uses, and recheck condition.

Historical rates may inform internal analysis only when authorized and comparable. They are not final market rates. Public procurement cases can support cost-realism reasoning but do not establish Saudi prices.
