# Media and Corporate Communications Module

## Activate for

Corporate communications, PR, media relations, editorial operations, executive communications, social publishing, photography, video, design, media-center operations, internal communications, and annual communication retainers.

## Scope ontology

Split the scope into explicit workstreams rather than one generic "communications management" line:

1. strategy and planning
2. editorial and copy operations
3. creative design
4. photo and video production
5. social publishing and community workflows
6. media relations and earned media
7. executive communications
8. internal communications
9. reporting and learning
10. digital asset management and archive

Activate only the workstreams required by the source set.

## Discovery

Resolve:
- audiences and stakeholder groups
- Arabic, English, and other language requirements
- channels and publishing ownership
- approval levels for ordinary, urgent, executive, and sensitive content
- expected content volume by format
- coverage locations and production windows
- source-file and archive requirements
- media-list ownership and journalist-contact rules
- response-time requirements
- copyright, music, photography, talent, and location permissions
- client-provided vs supplier-provided tools and accounts

Unknown quantities remain assumptions, not invented volumes.

## Operating model

For recurring retainers define:
- monthly planning rhythm
- weekly editorial check
- daily request intake
- urgent request path
- executive/sensitive escalation
- production booking process
- publishing authorization
- monthly reporting and learning loop

A content request should move through:
`brief -> fact check -> draft -> review -> approval -> production -> publish -> archive -> measurement`

## Deliverable unit catalog

| Deliverable | Typical unit | Acceptance criteria examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| editorial calendar | month | approved topics, channels, owners, dates |
| social copy | post | approved message, language, platform fit |
| design | design | approved dimensions, brand compliance, source file where contracted |
| video | video / finished minute | resolution, duration, captions, audio rights, approved cut |
| photo coverage | production day / selected image | minimum selected set, edit standard, metadata |
| press release | release | approved facts, Arabic/English where required, distribution list |
| media pitch | pitch / outreach cycle | target list, approved angle, outreach record |
| executive speech | speech | approved duration, audience, message architecture |
| media report | report | links, source classification, reach only where verified |
| archive handover | monthly / final package | naming taxonomy, final assets, source files per contract |

## Staffing logic

Common roles:
- account/project lead
- communications strategist
- Arabic editor/copywriter
- English editor where needed
- designer
- photographer/videographer
- editor/motion designer
- media relations lead
- publishing/community specialist
- QA/fact checker
- digital asset librarian for high-volume scopes

Do not promise unlimited volume through a fixed team without a capacity model.

## KPI families

Separate:
- delivery KPIs: on-time completion, approval cycle, urgent turnaround
- quality KPIs: correction rate, brand compliance, factual error rate
- channel KPIs: engagement, watch time, clicks, only where relevant
- earned-media KPIs: placements, quality/tier, message pull-through
- operational KPIs: backlog, revision cycles, SLA adherence

Do not guarantee reach or media placement when the supplier does not control it.

## Pricing drivers

Price recurring operations separately from variable production:
- retained team capacity
- per-output production
- travel/location
- equipment
- talent/voiceover
- licenses and stock
- translation
- printing
- after-hours surge
- third-party distribution or monitoring

Never bury cinematic production, unlimited urgent requests, or travel inside an undefined monthly fee.

## Cross-module interfaces

- campaign content -> Marketing & Promotion
- event coverage -> Events & Experiences
- issue escalation -> Crisis & Reputation
- website/content publishing -> Digital & AI
- media and sentiment feedback -> Monitoring & Intelligence

For each interface define producer, consumer, cadence, format, and approval owner.

## Failure modes

Block or revise when:
- volume is undefined but SLA is fixed
- client publishing access is assumed
- media placement is promised as guaranteed earned coverage
- rights ownership is unclear
- urgent path bypasses factual/legal approval
- archive/source-file ownership contradicts pricing
- bilingual production has no language QA owner
