# Financial Offer Outline

1. Offer identification
2. Currency and price basis
3. Client BOQ / price table
4. Optional items
5. Exclusions and client-supplied items
6. VAT presentation
7. Payment milestones or payment terms
8. Offer validity and supplier-price dependencies
9. Commercial assumptions
10. Signature / authorization fields when required

The client-facing offer must never include hidden internal cost or margin calculations.
