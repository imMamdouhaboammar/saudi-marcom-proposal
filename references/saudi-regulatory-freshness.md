# Saudi Regulatory Freshness

Use this file as a trigger map. Detailed state resolution lives in references/regulatory-state-resolver.md.

## Rule

For every current compliance statement record:
- authority
- instrument/service
- status
- publication date if known
- effective date if verified
- checked date
- official source
- exact technical or commercial implication

Do not cache a legal or regulatory conclusion as timeless proposal boilerplate.

## Government procurement

Government bids can depend on:
- the currently effective Government Tenders and Procurement Law and regulations
- Etimad submission behavior
- buyer addenda and mandatory forms
- local-content mechanisms
- guarantees/qualification requirements where named

A 2026 law announcement created transition risk. Resolve effective state per bid.

## Tax

Use current ZATCA law/regulation sources and buyer form treatment.

Do not hardcode 15% as an eternal invariant even though it is the current standard rate.

## Personal data

AI, CRM, monitoring, registration, audience profiling, employee tools, and analytics may trigger PDPL questions.

Map actual data flows first, including cross-border transfer.

## Media and advertising

Use the current General Authority for Media Regulation identity and current official service/regulation.

Do not use legacy authority naming from old proposals.

## Events

Classify the activity before selecting a permit service. Current GEA event and show services can have different requirements and lead times.

## Secondary sources

Secondary commentary is useful for issue spotting only. Material compliance claims should be verified against official primary sources.
