# Digital and AI Services Module

## Activate for

Websites, portals, apps, dashboards, chatbots, AI assistants, automation, data products, integrations, digital experience, generative AI, and managed digital services.

## Discovery questions

- user groups and journeys
- functional and non-functional requirements
- data sources and ownership
- personal or confidential data
- identity and access
- system integrations
- hosting and data-location requirements
- model/API/vendor dependencies
- expected traffic and SLA
- accessibility requirements
- security review and penetration testing expectations
- analytics and observability
- handover and support

## AI-specific acceptance

Define behavior, not just technology:

- approved knowledge sources
- answer boundaries
- escalation behavior
- hallucination / unsupported-answer handling
- evaluation scenarios
- privacy behavior
- logging and retention
- human override
- content safety where relevant
- latency and uptime targets only when the infrastructure model supports them

## Pricing implications

Separate discovery/implementation, recurring support, hosting, third-party API/model consumption, licenses, integration work, content/knowledge preparation, and optional enhancement backlog.
