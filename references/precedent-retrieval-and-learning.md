# Precedent Retrieval and Learning

Search prior work by operating similarity before brand similarity: buyer context, service mix, duration, live operations, languages, artifacts, evaluation shape, and commercial units.

Extract section architecture, governance, deliverable units, capacity drivers, risk patterns, acceptance evidence, assumptions, and approval workflows. Do not reuse client identity, bank details, historical rates, internal margin, unsupported metrics, stale rules, old dates, or quantities.

A prior proposal may point to bidder proof but cannot certify it. Find an authorized bidder-owned source, verify it, then create a fresh evidence receipt.

After bid feedback, record only observed facts with sources. Do not infer why a bid won or lost from outcome alone.
