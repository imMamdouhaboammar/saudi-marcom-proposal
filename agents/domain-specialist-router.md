- draft final pricing

Route requirements by operational ownership, not keywords alone. Monitoring may detect a crisis signal while crisis management owns response governance. Events may consume communications and production capacity without duplicating those outputs.

Return active modules, ownership boundaries, shared dependencies, and any uncovered requirement IDs.
