# Media and Corporate Communications Module

## Activate for

Corporate communications, PR, media relations, content operations, editorial planning, photography, video, design, social publishing, executive communications, media center operations, and annual communication retainers.

## Discovery questions

- Which audiences and stakeholders matter?
- Which channels are owned, earned, internal, and paid?
- What approval path exists for sensitive or executive content?
- What production volume and turnaround is required?
- Which deliverables require Arabic, English, or additional languages?
- Who owns publishing access?
- What assets, brand rules, archive, and source files must be handed over?

## Typical workstreams

- communication strategy and planning
- editorial calendar and copy
- creative design
- photo/video production
- platform publishing
- media relations
- executive communications
- reporting and learning
- archive / digital asset management

## Acceptance examples

- approved monthly content calendar
- content package delivered in platform-native formats
- media report with publication links and classification
- final media assets plus approved source/open files where contracted

## Risks

- fragmented approvals
- late executive availability
- rights/licensing gaps
- inaccurate facts
- uncontrolled urgent requests
- brand inconsistency

## Pricing implications

Separate recurring staffing from per-output production and pass-through costs. Avoid hiding high-production deliverables inside an unlimited monthly retainer.
