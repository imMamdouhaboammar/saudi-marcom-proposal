# Final Proposal QC

## Purpose

The final review should try to disprove submission readiness. It is not a proofreading pass.

## Severity

- `BLOCKER`: likely disqualification, materially wrong price, unsupported mandatory claim, missing mandatory requirement, or confidential data leakage
- `MAJOR`: material ambiguity, unpriced promise, weak scored evidence, schedule-resource inconsistency, or regulatory uncertainty that affects delivery
- `POLISH`: readability, wording, design consistency, or non-material improvement

## Gate 1: requirement coverage

- every mandatory requirement has a response and evidence
- evaluation criteria are explicitly mapped
- all required forms, declarations, certificates, CVs, and appendices are present or tracked
- issued addenda and clarification responses were incorporated

## Gate 2: claims

Search for:

- percentages
- market size or audience figures
- "largest", "leading", "first", or similar claims
- years of experience
- number of projects
- named clients
- certifications
- response-time promises
- performance targets
- permit or license claims

Each must be verified, buyer-stated, bidder-approved, benchmarked with a source, or removed/qualified.

## Gate 3: technical execution

- deliverables are countable or reviewable
- acceptance criteria exist where needed
- roles are assigned
- approvals are included in schedule logic
- urgent-request behavior is realistic
- Plan B exists for high-impact dependencies
- digital/AI/data scope states relevant access, privacy, and security assumptions
- event scope has supplier, venue, access, technical, and permit dependencies where relevant

## Gate 4: commercial reconciliation

- every priced line maps to scope
- every scope item maps to price, inclusion, exclusion, or client supply
- quantities and durations agree across files
- optional items do not silently enter the core total
- zero price is intentional
- VAT logic and total formulas work
- payment milestones total 100% when percentage-based
- commercial assumptions are visible

## Gate 5: confidentiality and contamination

Search the whole submission set for:

- names of past clients not intended for the current proposal
- old project titles
- old dates
- prior prices
- phone/email details from copied templates
- account or bank details
- tax identifiers
- comments, hidden sheets, speaker notes, or metadata that should not ship

## Gate 6: Arabic and visual integrity

- RTL renders correctly
- headings and body hierarchy are consistent
- tables are not clipped
- footnotes remain legible
- Arabic/English line wrapping is correct
- numbers and units are unambiguous
- the financial workbook can print/export cleanly

## Gate 7: submission package

- filenames match buyer instructions
- versions are final and dated
- signatures/stamps are handled as required
- portal file-size or format constraints are respected
- no internal-only workbook or confidential cost sheet is accidentally included

## Final status

- `READY`: no unresolved blocker and all mandatory proof current
- `READY WITH DISCLOSED ASSUMPTIONS`: no blocker, but explicit assumptions remain that the buyer/user accepts
- `NOT READY`: blocker remains

Do not use a numeric readiness score to disguise a blocker.
