# Evaluator Simulation

For every disclosed criterion preserve the buyer wording, locate the answer, locate the proof, test support, record contradictions, and ask the smallest question that exposes a gap.

Review pass/fail requirements separately from scored criteria. A polished narrative cannot compensate for a missing mandatory form or attachment.

When scoring is opaque, do not create a scoring model. Use labeled heuristics such as findability, specificity, evidence proximity, execution clarity, risk visibility, acceptance clarity, and technical-financial consistency.

Any material addendum or change to criteria, requirement, deliverable, SLA, staffing, language, geography, price basis, or form makes the report stale.
